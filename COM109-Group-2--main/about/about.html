ABOUT US

Our story, our mission, our community.

Our Story

Iron Peak Fitness was founded in 2018 in the heart of Belfast with one simple goal — to create a gym that feels welcoming to everyone, regardless of experience or fitness level.

What started as a small weights room has grown into a full-service fitness facility with modern equipment, expert coaches, group classes, and a genuine community of members who support each other every day.

Our Mission

💪 Strength

We help our members build physical and mental strength through structured training and expert guidance.

🤝 Community

We foster a supportive environment where everyone belongs — from beginners to competitive athletes.

📈 Progress

We believe in steady, sustainable progress. No shortcuts, no gimmicks — just hard work and consistency.

  • Group fitness classes for all levels
