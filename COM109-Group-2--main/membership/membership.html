Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my membership later?

Yes. Logged-in members can change their plan from the Manage Account page.

Is there a joining fee?

No. There is no joining fee for any of the memberships shown above.

How old do I need to be?

Members must be at least 16 years old to create an account.

Can I cancel or freeze my plan?

Yes. Plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice or temporarily frozen.
